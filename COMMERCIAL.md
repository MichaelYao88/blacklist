BLACKLIST is **source-available**, not open source. The free license

educational, research, hobby, and other noncommercial use.

**Commercial use requires a separate written commercial license.**

## What counts as commercial use

Commercial use includes, without limitation:

- selling the game or a modified version of it
- paid builds, paid access, subscriptions, or paid hosting
- ad-supported or otherwise monetized distribution
- bundling the game into a commercial product or service
- internal use by a for-profit business in connection with its business
- distributing a modified version as a competing product

## Available terms

Commercial terms are negotiable. Typical structures include:

- a flat commercial license fee
- a revenue-share agreement, for example a percentage of gross revenue above
  an agreed threshold
- a per-product or per-title license
- a custom agreement for a publisher, platform, or studio

The exact fee, revenue share, reporting period, audit rights, territory, term,
and termination rights are set only in a signed commercial agreement. Nothing
in this document is itself a license or an offer capable of acceptance.

## Music is separate

The commercial license covers the **code** in this repository. It does not
cover the third-party music. The Liborio Conti and Sub_Clair tracks remain
under their own licenses and cannot be sublicensed, sold, or redistributed as
standalone audio. A commercial agreement for the game does not transfer any
rights to that music.

## Inquiries

Open an issue at <https://github.com/MichaelYao88/blacklist/issues> with the
subject **Commercial license** and include:

- who you are and the entity that would use the game
- what you want to do commercially
- whether you want a flat license or revenue share
- the expected scale and term
